# Ayurvedic

Free old English copies of classic Ayurvedic books, plus free modern medical guides and research about acidity.

Each book has two kinds of files:

- **PDF**: the scanned book pages. You can search inside them.
- **TXT**: plain text that a computer read from the scans. It has some spelling mistakes, but it is easy to search.

### What each book is about

- **Charaka Samhita**: the most important book of Ayurveda. It is about general medicine: what causes disease, how to find it, how to treat it, food, daily habits and herbs.
- **Sushruta Samhita**: the second big book. It is about surgery, plus many treatments.
- **The Materia Medica of the Hindus**: herbs and medicines, and what they are used for.

## Copyright

- **Charaka (Kaviratna)**, **Sushruta** and **The Materia Medica of the Hindus** are more than 100 years old. Everyone is free to use them (public domain).
- **Charaka 1949** is free to read on archive.org, in the Digital Library of India collection. It may still be under copyright in some countries. If the rights owner objects, remove it.
- **NIH guides** (NIDDK, NCCIH, ODS) are US government works and free to copy, if NIH is named as the source (<https://www.niddk.nih.gov/copyright>).
- **Research papers and guidelines**: free to share for non-commercial use, under the license shown in their tables. Do not change them.
